About

I'm an artist and designer working across lighting design, installation, and visual art. I create experiences through light, space, and interaction, transforming ideas into immersive environments and tangible forms.

With a background in theatre and scenography, I am currently expanding my practice into creative technology and human-computer interaction, exploring new ways for people, systems, and environments to communicate and respond to one another.
