Light Box 2026

Interactive installation

This is an interactive installation exploring the relationship between sound, light, and water.

Sound vibrations create changing patterns on a shallow water surface, while a ring of LED lights illuminates and transforms what we see. The system is housed in a small black-box structure inspired by theatre, with an opening that allows viewers to lean in and observe the water up close.
