2 to 1024

Interactive installation

2 to 1024 is an interactive installation exploring the intersections of female labor, textiles, embodiment, and computation.

The interaction begins with a 3×3 perforated matrix, where audiences must bend down, insert their hands into a small opening, and thread metal-wrapped yarn through holes. Each hole represents a discrete input signal, and this accumulative threading acts as the primary computational input. The main visual output is an LED-integrated tapestry created through "arm knitting." This specific technique constrains the distance between the arms, forcing the body to operate within a fixed range, physically reflecting the instrumentalization of the female body.
